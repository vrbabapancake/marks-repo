Extensions and phone trees

Each comma in a phone number adds a one-second delay. Use commas to reach a direct extension or move through a phone tree.

415-555-5555,1234Dials the number, waits one second, then dials 1234.
415-555-5555,,,1234Dials the number, waits three seconds, then dials 1234.
415-555-5555,,,,,,Dials the number, waits six seconds, then leaves a message.

Limits

Up to 10 contact methods per user, including no more than three phone numbers for voice and three for SMS.

Formatting

Toll-free numbers are not supported. Remove trunk prefixes, for example +44 1332 412251 rather than 01332 412 251.

Test it

Click Test next to a contact method to confirm the connection without creating an incident.

Notification Rules

Notification rules determine how you are notified when an incident is assigned to you, covering both the frequency of notifications and the methods used. You can configure different sets of rules for high- and low-urgency incidents, and for when incidents change.

!
High-urgency rules are created for you

After you add a contact method, high-urgency notification rules are automatically configured. You can edit or delete them at any time.

  1. Navigate to your Notification Rules tab. See Notification Rules.
  2. Under High urgency incident notifications, click +.
  3. Enter the number of minutes to wait before notifying. The default, 0, notifies immediately.
  4. Select the contact method. You can reuse a method in several rules.
  5. Click Save.
!
Going on vacation?

Create a schedule override so someone covers your shifts. Deleting your notification rules stops notifications, but incidents can still be assigned to you while you remain on call. It does not remove your contact methods.
