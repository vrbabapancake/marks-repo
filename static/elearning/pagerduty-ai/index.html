PagerDuty's AI Capabilities

PagerDuty's AI capabilities are a set of generative and agentic AI features embedded across the PD Reliability Platform. From AI agents handling well-understood tasks to generative AI that summarizes key information about an incident, PagerDuty's AI capabilities augment, guide, and resolve operational work so your teams can scale and build operational resilience.

Two kinds of AI

Generative AI creates content for you: incident summaries, status update drafts, post-incident review narratives, and automation log digests. It saves responders the time spent writing and keeps stakeholders informed.

Examples in PagerDuty
PagerDuty AssistantStatus UpdatesPost-Incident ReviewsAutomation Digest

Agentic AI takes on work for you. AI agents handle tedious or repetitive tasks within your connected tools, such as transcribing incident calls, finding shift coverage, or pulling logs during triage, freeing people for high-impact work.

Examples in PagerDuty
Insights AgentShift AgentScribe AgentPaige (SRE Agent)

Generative AI is predictive, and sometimes its output is misleading or false. Always fact-check AI output before you use it. PagerDuty does not use your data to train the models used for PagerDuty's AI capabilities.

The PagerDuty Assistant

The PagerDuty Assistant is a generative AI chatbot that gives responders helpful insight from event to resolution. During an incident, mention @PagerDuty in Slack, or @PagerDuty advance in Microsoft Teams, and ask a question in plain language.

It anticipates common diagnostic questions, suggests troubleshooting steps, and routes your request to the right specialized AI agent. The goal: a lower mean time to resolution (MTTR) for critical, unplanned work.

Enabling and Governing PagerDuty's AI Capabilities

Admins control PagerDuty's AI capabilities from one place: AI → AI Settings. Setup is quick at a high level, and access can be rolled out gradually.

Connect Slack or Microsoft Teams so the Assistant and agents can work where responders collaborate.

The PagerDuty MCP Server

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI assistants and agents connect to the tools your teams use. The PagerDuty MCP Server brings PagerDuty data and actions into AI tools like Claude, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot, so teams can work with incidents, services, and schedules without switching tools.

The Remote MCP Server is hosted by PagerDuty, and all new tools and capabilities will land there.
